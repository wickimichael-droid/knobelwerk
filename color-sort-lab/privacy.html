Privacy Policy – Color Sort Lab

for the app “Color Sort Lab” (Android) · Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Deutsche Fassung

In short: Color Sort Lab needs no account and never asks for your name or email address. Your progress stays on your phone. We use Google services for ads, analytics and crash reports. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Optionen (Options) → Datenschutz-Optionen (privacy options).

1. Controller

Knobelwerk, Michael Wicki, Bachmättli 7A, 6280 Hochdorf, Switzerland
Email: support@knobelwerk.ch

This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data the app processes

2.1 Progress on your device

Progress, stars, coins, purchased designs and settings are stored only on your device. If Android backup is enabled, Android may back up your progress to your Google account and restore it on a new device. You control this backup in your Android settings; we have no access to it.

2.2 Advertising – Google AdMob

The app shows ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA). This may involve processing your device’s advertising ID, IP address (also used to estimate approximate location), device type, operating system, language, diagnostic data and interactions with ads. Purposes: delivering ads, measuring reach, preventing fraud and – only with your consent – personalized advertising.

Consent: In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the app shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) at launch. If you decline, you only receive limited, non-personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2.3 Analytics – Google Analytics for Firebase

To understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard), the app sends events to Google Analytics for Firebase: level start and completion with moves, stars and play time, hints used, undone moves, ads viewed, purchases (product only, no payment data), daily bonus and unlocked worlds, plus technical data such as a random app instance ID, device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate country. We do not link this data to your name or email address.

Collection starts only after the consent form has been answered. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the app applies your choice (Google Consent Mode). Event data is kept by Google for no more than 14 months.

2.4 Crash and error reports – Firebase Crashlytics

If the app crashes or a program error occurs, it sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics: error location, device model, Android version, app version, device state at the time of the error, a random installation ID, the affected level and the last game actions before the error. We use these reports only to fix bugs. Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days.

2.5 Purchases – Google Play

In-app purchases (coin packs, “No Ads”, starter pack) are handled exclusively by Google Play. We receive no payment data, only confirmation of which product was bought and a purchase token so the app can unlock it. Google’s privacy policy applies.

3. Legal bases

Personalized ads, analytics and the related storage on your device are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalized ads fund the free game and crash reports keep it stable; both are based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Purchases are based on our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Transfers to the USA

Google may process data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses also apply.

5. Children

Color Sort Lab is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time.

  • Change consent: in the app under Optionen (Options) → Datenschutz-Optionen (privacy options).
  • Delete data: uninstall the app and reset your advertising ID in Android settings. Because we don’t know you by name, we can only delete data at Google if you tell us your app instance ID – email us at support@knobelwerk.ch.
  • Complaints: to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your EU data protection authority.

7. Changes

If the app changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@knobelwerk.ch
